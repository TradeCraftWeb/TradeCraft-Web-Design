About Us

The Hopgood Family

Proud Lincolnshire locals helping trades build a stronger online presence.

We are the Hopgood family — a family of four based in Stickford, Lincolnshire. With over 15 years of experience in the IT industry, we decided to create TradeCraft Web Design to fill a real gap in our local community.

We saw too many hardworking plumbers, electricians, builders, roofers, and other tradespeople missing out on valuable work simply because they didn't have a website that worked as hard as they do. So we built a family business dedicated to solving that problem.

We don't just design websites — we create practical, fast-loading, mobile-friendly websites that help local trades generate more enquiries and grow their businesses.
